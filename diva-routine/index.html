Windows app · free

Routines for sport, workouts and dance, from your videos

Collect training, sport and dance videos in a visual library, mark the moves you want on a timeline, and Diva Routine draws each one as an illustrated sequence of key phases. Build routines with reps and notes, then train along with guided video sessions or prepare full classes from playlists of videos, music and other files.

What it does

From a pile of videos to a printed routine or a ready-to-play class, without drawing a single figure yourself.

🗂️

Visual library

Folders with mosaics of what is inside, drag and drop, and custom names.

📲

Import from social networks

Links, playlists and saved collections from Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and Facebook, with step-by-step tutorials. Linked lists stay in sync.

🎞️

Zoomable timeline

Zoom in, pick the exact stretch and see a clear preview of its frames before extracting.

✏️

Illustrated phases

Pose detection splits each move into key phases and draws them as clean poster-style illustrations.

📋

Routines

Drag exercise sequences into a routine, organise routines and sequences in tabs and folders, add reps and notes, improve the drawings with AI, and print or save an A4 PDF sheet.

▶️

Guided video sessions

Play a workout as a session: videos, routines and images one after another in full screen, with speed, volume, skip and timers to train along.

🎶

Class playlists

Prepare whole classes as playlists that mix videos, routines, Spotify music, audio, images and other files, and play them from start to finish. Long-press any item under the player and drag it to change the order.

☁️

Your data, everywhere

Sign in with Google and your library syncs through your own Google Drive across computers. You can also connect MEGA to import your videos and keep a copy there.

🤝

Shared with your team

Share folders, routines and drawings by Gmail; collaborators can add videos and edit routines.

Get Diva Routine

Download Diva-Routine-Setup.exe from the latest release and run it. It installs for your user only, without admin rights, and updates itself: when a new version is out, it downloads in the background and asks you to restart.

Download Diva-Routine-Setup.exe Source code on GitHub

Web version (beta): open Diva Routine in your browser, no install needed. Works on computers and phones. Your videos and routines are saved in that browser and, if you sign in with Google, synced with your Drive and shared folders; Premium and social imports are available in the Windows app for now.

The app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time: click “More info → Run anyway”. Your videos and routines stay on your computer and, if you sign in, in your own Google Drive.
