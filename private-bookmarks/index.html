What it does

Your bookmarks bar stays the same — only the bookmarks you pick ask for the PIN.

🔒

PIN on bookmarks

Choose which bookmarks ask for a PIN before opening. Everything else works as usual.

📁

Hidden folders

Hide the contents of a whole folder. Only “Protected folder” shows until you enter the PIN.

🎨

Your choice of icon

A blue padlock or the site's original icon: you decide how each protected bookmark looks.

🗂️

Encrypted backup

Download a copy of everything you protect, encrypted with your PIN, so you never lose anything.

☁️

Sync

Take your protected bookmarks to another computer through your Chrome account — always encrypted.
